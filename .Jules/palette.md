## 2024-07-03 - Tooltip for Animation Detail Close Button
**Learning:** Icon-only close buttons in secondary panels (like the Animation details pane) often lack `aria-label` and `title` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers and lacking hover context for mouse users.
**Action:** When adding or modifying close buttons, ensure `aria-label` and `title` attributes are set using localized strings to maintain accessibility and usability parity.
