## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon-only Toolbar Buttons in Netmonitor
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like those for 'Clear', 'Toggle Recording', 'Search', 'Blocking', and 'HTTP Custom Request') in the DevTools Netmonitor toolbar heavily rely on `title` attributes for tooltips but lack `aria-label` attributes for proper screen reader accessibility. Although standard convention suggests `title` can provide an accessible name, having explicit `aria-label`s mapping to the same localized strings is a best practice for consistent screen reader experiences and aligns with other parts of the UI.
**Action:** Add `aria-label` matching the localized text used in `title` for icon-only buttons (`Clear`, `ToggleRecording`, `Blocking`, `Search`, `HTTPCustomRequest`) in `devtools/client/netmonitor/src/components/Toolbar.js`.
