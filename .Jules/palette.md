## 2024-10-24 - Missing Tooltip on Settings Button in Responsive Design Mode Toolbar
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons without explicit labels or tooltips are a common accessibility oversight. Adding a localized title acts as a tooltip providing important context for users, especially those using screen readers or relying on visual hover cues.
**Action:** Always check icon-only buttons for `title` or `aria-label` attributes and ensure they are localized appropriately to improve accessibility and micro-UX.
